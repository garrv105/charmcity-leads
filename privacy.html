Privacy Policy

Charm City Lead Response (operated by Garrv Sipani, Sole Proprietor) · Baltimore, Maryland · Effective 31 October 2026

Who we are

Charm City Lead Response ("we", "us") provides automated lead-response services to local service businesses. This policy describes what information we collect through our website and messaging, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

When you submit our contact form or call our number, we collect the information you provide: your name, mobile phone number, email address, and a description of the service you need. We also record the date and time of your request and the channel it arrived through (website or phone).

How we use it

  • To send you a one-time SMS and email confirming we received your request and telling you when to expect a callback.
  • To contact you by phone, text or email to schedule or carry out the service you asked about.
  • To keep an internal log of requests so nothing is missed.

SMS / text messaging

By providing your mobile number and checking the consent box on our form, you agree to receive transactional text messages from Charm City Lead Response related to your request. Message frequency varies and is typically 1–3 messages per request. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out; reply HELP for assistance. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of any purchase.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Mobile information and SMS consent are not shared with any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Who we share information with

We use service providers solely to deliver our service: a messaging carrier (Twilio) to send texts, an email provider to send email, and a workflow and spreadsheet platform to log requests. These providers process data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes. We do not sell personal information.

Retention

We keep request records for as long as needed to provide the service and for our business records, and delete them on request.

Your choices

You may opt out of texts by replying STOP. You may ask us to correct or delete your information at any time by emailing the address below.

Changes
